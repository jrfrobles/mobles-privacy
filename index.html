Privacy Policy

Mobles Construction Services · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Mobles Construction Services ("we", "us", "our") operates an internal software tool (the "App") that connects to Meta's Facebook platform to manage our own Facebook Page and advertising. This policy explains what information the App handles and how we use it.

1. Who this applies to

The App is used only by Mobles Construction Services to manage our own Facebook Page and ad account. It is not offered to the public, and there is no public sign-up or login. This policy also covers people who interact with our Facebook Page or ads (for example, by commenting, reacting, or sending us a message).

2. Information we access

Through Meta's APIs, and only within the permissions we have granted, the App may access:

  • Our Page content: posts, photos, and Page details.
  • Public interactions with our Page: comments, reactions, and the public name and profile identifier Meta provides for the person who made them.
  • Messages people send to our Page, if they contact us through Messenger.
  • Aggregated insights and ad performance data: reach, impressions, clicks, and similar statistics. Meta provides these as totals and does not identify individuals.

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, or any information from your Facebook account beyond what Meta makes available to Page owners.

3. How we use information

  • To publish and schedule posts and ads on our Page.
  • To read and respond to comments and messages, including inquiries and estimate requests.
  • To understand how our posts and ads perform so we can improve them.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share it with third parties, except: (a) with Meta, as part of using its platform; (b) with service providers who help us run the App under confidentiality obligations; or (c) when the law requires it.

5. Storage and security

Access credentials are stored securely on our own devices with restricted access. Information retrieved from Meta is used to carry out the tasks above and is not kept longer than needed for them.

6. Retention

We keep messages and inquiries only as long as needed to respond to you and run our business, or as the law requires. Data held by Meta, such as your comments on our Page, is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To request deletion:

  1. Send a message to our Facebook Page or email us at jrfrobles@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your name as it appears on Facebook.
  3. We will delete any related information we hold within 30 days and confirm with you.

You can also delete your own comments on our Page at any time through Facebook. If you are in the Philippines, you have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

8. Children

Our Page and App are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
